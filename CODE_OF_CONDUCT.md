# Community Conduct

Grom Synthetic BR welcomes constructive participation from researchers, developers and students.

Please:

- communicate respectfully;
- focus criticism on methods, evidence and code;
- document uncertainty and limitations;
- preserve attribution, licensing and provenance;
- avoid submitting private, confidential or restricted material;
- keep technical discussion reproducible and evidence-based.

Disagreement is welcome when it improves the research. Maintainers may reject contributions that do not follow these guidelines or that create unresolved legal, privacy or provenance concerns.
